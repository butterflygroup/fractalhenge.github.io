The Ennead of Heliopolis

The Ennead of Heliopolis is the Great or Heliopolitan Ennead: a traditional grouping of nine deities centered on the solar cult of Heliopolis (Egyptian Iunu), one of the major centers of ancient Egyptian theology. In Heliopolitan creation thought, these gods sit at the head of a divine lineage that orders the emergence and continuation of the world.

The English word Ennead comes from Greek enneás (“nine”), echoing the Egyptian term Pesedjet (“the Nine”), the usual name for this council of gods.

The Nine Deities

The standard members form a generational family descending from the creator god:

Deity Role / Domain
Atum (or Atum-Re / Ra-Atum) Self-created sun god and creator of the universe
Shu God of air, wind, and separation
Tefnut Goddess of moisture, rain, and dew
Geb God of the earth
Nut Goddess of the sky
Osiris God of the afterlife, resurrection, and fertility
Isis Goddess of magic, motherhood, and healing
Set (Seth) God of chaos, storms, and the desert
Nephthys Goddess of mourning, protection, and night
